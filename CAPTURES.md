# Capture notes

Reviewed for publication on 5 October 2026. These images illustrate the projects; they are not production health checks.

- Web interfaces were captured from local copies. Live accounts, Discord services, private data stores and physical devices were not connected.
- SheetParcel and Contractstudio use their bundled fictional examples.
- KPI dashboard captures use a disconnected source state; employee names and source URLs in the capture-only requirements were replaced with example values.
- Metis, Zagreus and Prometheus use empty local fixtures.
- Documentation captures are screenshots of newly authored public-safe portfolio notes, grounded in the inspected source and project documentation. They are not screenshots of a running app or original repository pages.
- Native desktop captures were attempted, but the capture tool failed to activate the windows and included an obstructing desktop overlay. Those captures were excluded.
- Pico previews were rendered by the firmware tooling in an earlier project session; they are not photographs or evidence of a current physical deployment.
- No original private application source or customer/campaign records are included.
